What a copied screen can access

A custom UI runs isolated: it cannot read your sign-in, your other tabs, or anything outside this command center. It acts as you, in this command center only: it can read your command center's name, your agents, this conversation, your automations, runs and shared files, send messages, wake your own automations, and ask to change which conversation design answers you.

What it can read, it could keep. Someone else's UI is someone else's code: running it shows it this conversation. Switch back to default chat to stop that. Visual previews have no access to your data.

          Before voice starts

          Voice will use the voice connection that you bound to this command center. Your microphone audio goes directly to that service, and any use belongs to that resource's account. TinyAssets never substitutes a shared credential. TinyAssets keeps the words you send and your agent's text reply in this conversation, but does not store the raw audio.

          Your browser or device speech service will turn your microphone audio into text and read your agent's reply aloud. Depending on your browser, speech recognition may send audio to the browser vendor and may not work offline. TinyAssets sends the recognized text through the same conversation as typed chat and keeps that text exchange, but TinyAssets does not receive or store the raw audio.
