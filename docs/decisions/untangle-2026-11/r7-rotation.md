# r7-rotation as built (2026-11-15)

Lane R's key rotation, PR 1 of two: the owner's answers a rotation's driver reads, and the operator ops that ask them. The design was researched before the build (Fact 14; lane R's research of 2026-11-15, item 2, on `origin/main` at `29c9f1bc1`). PR 2, the kit's driver, is `untangle/r7-rotation-driver` (item 6).

1. **One predicate, `left`: what the sweep could not bring under the answer's kid.**
   - `dataKeyRewrap` answers `{rewrapped, roots, left, kid}`. `kid` is the kid of the object's wrapped data key, read from the key's own envelope header (`parseBlob`), null when the object holds none. `left` counts each root found that is not under that kid.
   - `gatewayReseal` answers `{resealed, left, kid}`. `kid` is the gateway's active kid as its last reseal answered it, null when the object holds no blob. `left` counts each blob not under it. So a ring changed mid-sweep leaves the blobs sealed before the change counted too, rather than read as done.
   - The driver asks again until `left` is 0 for every owner, and its kid check (PR 2) compares `kid` with the stored ring's active kid.
2. **A root a pass wrote again is counted, never refused.** `FILES.rewrapManifestRoot` answers `{ key: null }` when the root's etag changed between its read and its put, leaving the root as the pass wrote it (a pass always writes the active kid). The transient `root_changed` refusal is gone from `REFUSALS`: nothing else threw it.
3. **A restore's hold is counted, never refused** (replacing `r7-sweep.md` item 8's refusal, #5616).
   - While a restore holds the owner, `dataKeyRewrap` touches nothing and answers `left` = the record's distinct generations (`heldGenerations`, `restore.ts`: `gen`, and the pin's `fresh` when set). The safety of item 8 holds: no pass reads a held owner done.
   - Through the operator channel the transient `restoring` was thrown so the queue retried it, then dead-lettered, and the kit's ask stopped after `WAIT_MS`: one held owner stopped the sweep of every owner. Now it is answered at once.
   - The hold is read in the door's unit and the rewrap runs after it, as before. A pin landing between pins a generation the rewrap covers or writes a fresh one under the active kid; an R8 restore from an older copy landing between is what PR 2's second pass is for (item 6).
4. **The gap: doorbell subscriptions.** `wake_callbacks.blob`, sealed under a `GATEWAY_KEYRING` kid, was resealed only when rung (`wakes.ts`), and nothing called `GATEWAY.resealDoorbell`, so retiring a gateway kid broke every idle subscription.
   - `Doorbell.reseal` (`wakes.ts`, beside the ring's own reseal) reseals each subscription's blob through `GATEWAY.resealDoorbell`, each stored in a unit of its own by `ledger.resealed`. `gatewayReseal` folds its blobs and the connections' into one answer (`resealAnswer`, `contract/src/owner.ts`, beside `ResealAnswer`: in `connections.ts` it would be a new `wakes.ts` edge the policy-boundary guard refuses).
   - A `previous_blob` still inside its window (`previous_until` past now) is not resealed: it is bound to its own version and lapses by itself. It counts under its own kid, read from its header, until then.
   - A blob the gateway refuses (one that no longer opens or passes its record, which a ring answers `refused` and never sends) is counted left as it is, and the other subscriptions are swept all the same (review).
5. **Found while building: the doorbell's compare-and-swap lost a refresh.** `ledger.resealed` stored a reseal `WHERE version < ?`, but every subscribe and refresh stores its new secret at `DOORBELL_VERSION` (1) again (`doors/src/connect.ts`). So a ring's reseal of the old secret, landing after a refresh, overwrote the refreshed one; the sweep would make that likelier. It now stores only over the blob and version it was resealed from, as the connections' swap does (`AND blob = ?`), and answers whether it did. The ring's path takes the same fix.
6. **Two operator ops, `owner.rewrap` and `owner.reseal`,** built like `owner.state` and `owner.erase`: no args, in `OPERATOR_OPS` and the doors' `OPS`, each through `bindPlatformFor` to the reserved door (`dataKeyRewrap`, `gatewayReseal`), now in `OwnerDoors` with their answer types (`RewrapAnswer`, `ResealAnswer`). No confirm words: they change no meaning, only the kid a blob is under. Being `owner.` ops without words, mode `owner` takes `--act rewrap` and `--act reseal` for one owner. The answer is names, codes and counts.
   - **Owed, PR 2 (`untangle/r7-rotation-driver`):** kit mode `rotation` (`--keyring`), paging the directory's `o:` owners in batches, with its progress record in the CONTROL namespace (`deploy:rotation:<KEYRING>:<active kid>`: the ring's kids with their keys' hashes, and each pass's `began`, `through`, `owing` and `swept`, every time an operator answer's `done`), the kid check, and mode `retire` with the founder's words: for `GATEWAY_KEYRING` after one swept pass; for `FILES_KEYRING` only after a second pass begun `KEEP_DAYS` + 1 days after the first off-account copy night that followed the first pass. Also the check that no imported owner remains without an `o:` record after the window.
7. **No owner migration.** No schema changes: `previous_blob`'s kid is read from its header rather than stored.
8. **The proof.** On the fake clock and records only.
   - `export.test.ts`: a root the files Worker answers changed is counted `left` 1, and asked again it is 0; `kid` is the files ring's active kid, the one the rewrapped key is under.
   - `restore.test.ts` (rewritten from #5616's refusal): pinned, wiped and restoring, the rewrap answers `left` 2 (the generation and the pin's) and sends nothing to the files Worker; once done, it answers `left` 0 over the restored generation.
   - `doorbell.test.ts`: an idle subscription sealed under `k1` and never rung is resealed by the sweep; its next ring is sent, and the blob the object handed the gateway for it opens under the ring with `k1` retired (the gateway's own open, `gateway-support.ts`); a refresh landing while the gateway reseals is kept and counted left; the secret it replaced is counted left until its window lapses.
   - `connections.test.ts`: the connections' answers name the kid.
   - `chunks.test.ts`: a raced root answers `{ key: null }` and stays as written.
   - `doors/test/operator.test.ts`: both ops answer counts and a kid alone; a held owner's rewrap is answered and acknowledged, not retried; delivered again after the op ran and before its answer, each changes nothing more; a forged or unsigned message is dropped with nothing written. `doors-live.test.ts`: both answer through the channel, and the owner's own binding is refused `PLATFORM_ONLY` at both doors.
   - `deploy.test.ts`: `--act rewrap` and `--act reseal` are sent between two `owner.state`.
   - **Mutations**, each failing its test: the doorbell's half left out of `gatewayReseal` (the blob it rings with no longer opens once `k1` is retired); the swap back on the version alone (the refresh is overwritten); the replaced secret not counted; a changed root not counted; the kid read from the key before the rewrap; the hold not counted; `owner.rewrap` missing from `OPERATOR_OPS`.
