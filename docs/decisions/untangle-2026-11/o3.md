# O3 as built (2026-11-17)

B5's object half (d2 B5; `tasks.md` §10, lane O, the C4 and O2c lines): the owner object parses every door's binding with the contract's parser of its kind, picked by the kinds its row's caller takes, and a handler reads the binding as parsed. The contract's kinds and the binder's half were C4 and D3 (`c4.md`, `d3.md`).

## The plan (research, before building)

**What is there.** `OwnerDO.#bind(binding, door, sandbox)` takes a door class (`admission` and `import` among them) and a seven-value mode (`none`, `egress`, `sync`, `run`, `inbound`, `erasure`, `discard`), which restate the row's caller. It checks the binding's shape by hand, field by field: `importBinding`'s `keyed` join, the sandbox binding's `keyed` join and `owner/src/egress.ts`'s `sandboxBinding`, a copy of the contract's `sandboxToken` that six readers call again after the bind (C4's owed line). The account step checks `origin`, `authenticated_at` and the vendor again, and the inbound, reserved and erasure cases each check their binding's fields after the bind. The two unbound doors' methods hand the runner `undefined as unknown as Binding`, and their steps say `"caller": "none"` beside the contract's `UNBOUND_DOORS`, held equal by `stepProblems` (O2c's owed line). `BINDING_PARSERS` is used by the binder alone.

**The shape chosen** (d2 B5, Design, bullet 1):
1. **The caller names its binding kinds.** `owner/src/door-steps.ts` `CALLER_BINDINGS` maps each caller to the kinds it takes: the account callers the owner's account, its session and a run; the file readers those and the sync agent; the platform's doors the reserved kind; the import's the import kind; the sandbox doors their token's kind; `inbound` an inbound route; `none` no binding. `CallerKind` is its keys, so a caller and its kinds are one fact.
2. **The kind a binding presents is the contract's.** `bindingKind(value)` (`contract/src/binding.ts`) names it by the field that kind alone carries; `BINDING_PARSERS` parses it. The sandbox kind splits in two, `egress` and `sync`: a door takes one token or the other, and with one kind the object needed a token-kind mode again. The `erasure` entry, an alias of `reserved` no binder built, goes. `none` joins, with its parser.
3. **The bind step parses once.** `#bind(binding, caller, row)` refuses an erased, discarded or erasing owner first, as today; then the binding's kind among its caller's (`notTaken`, each refusal the one today's code gave), parsed by its parser; then the owner, its restore, its admission and a sandbox's lease. It returns the parsed binding, which the account, run, sync and runner steps read, and which the call carries (`Call.binding`, typed by the caller: `BindingFor<K>`). The hand checks go, and so does `egress.ts`'s copy: the egress handlers read `binding.sandbox` typed as the egress token.
4. **`none` is the contract's.** The two unbound doors' steps name no caller; the runner's row is `none` where `UNBOUND_DOORS` names it (`withSteps`), and `stepProblems` refuses a caller on an unbound door and its absence on any other. Their methods hand the runner `undefined`, which the `none` parser holds.

**Size.** The runner's bind and account steps (about 400 lines), the contract's kinds (80), the handlers that read a binding (100), the tests whose malformed bindings now meet the parser's words (100): about 900, one PR.

## As built

1. **`CALLER_BINDINGS`** (`owner/src/door-steps.ts`), each list frozen for the module-state guard. `TakenBy<K>` is a caller's kinds and `Binds` every caller but `none`.
2. **`contract/src/binding.ts`:** `EgressBinding`, `SyncBinding` and their parsers in place of `SandboxBinding`; `noBinding`; `BindingOf<K>`, which the binder's own `Kind<K>` copy gives way to; `bindingKind`. `sandboxToken` is the module's own. The binder builds `egress` and `sync` (`bindSandbox`, `bindSync`); its kinds exclude `none`.
3. **`#bind`** returns `HeldBy<K>`, the parsed binding of a kind `K` takes. The runner's mode vocabulary, `importBinding`, the sandbox, inbound and reserved shape checks, the account step's origin, sign-in time and vendor checks, `outsideOrigin`'s call and `owner/src/egress.ts` `sandboxBinding` are gone. "This object is not named by an owner" is said once, by the object's name reader, now a refusal (it was an `Error` at the internal readers).
4. **`Call.binding`** is the parsed binding, typed `BindingFor<K>`: each kind read as a `Binding` (a field it lacks is absent). `Door.binding` goes: a `units` handler reads the binding of the call it checks (`files.ts`, `runs.ts`, `relay.ts`, `signins.ts`, `codemode.ts`). A call of no named caller is a binding door's; `sandboxBilled` and `sandboxRung` name `none`. The run door's `door.binding.run as string` casts go.
5. **The import kind** is a kind like the others: its caller's mismatch and its parser are the general step's. Its callers' code (`#discarded`, the rehearsal branch, the `import` class's pending check) stays in `owner-do.ts`, where the cutover manifest names it (`cutover.json`, the `owner-do.ts` entry). **Departs from d2 B5, Design, bullet 2** (moving that code into `owner/src/import.ts`): the code is cutover-only, so it takes the importer's rule (`untangle-2026-11-remaining-build.md` §A5: no reshaping beyond what the row table needs), and the manifest's entry already deletes it in one place. B24's deletion removes it.

## What a caller sees

The recorders (`npm run record`, base `0baf94b9d`, and before it `1a796a084`, alike): `wire` and `tools` the same; `rpc` and `rv` differ in 6,073 of 85,362 lines, every one a refusal that stays a refusal, no answer that succeeded changed. Each is a binding no binder makes, or one of a kind its door does not take:
- **A malformed binding is its kind parser's `invalid`** (`invalid account binding`, `invalid reserved binding`, `invalid import binding`), not the object's own words (`invalid outside origin`, `authenticated_at is a time in whole seconds`, `invalid outside client vendor`, an import or platform binding's `refused`). A malformed session field is invalid before any session is looked for, so it leaves no refusal receipt, as any binding refused before its account.
- **A binding of a kind its door does not take is refused before the owner's state**: an owner's session at a sandbox door of a suspended or pending owner answers `SANDBOX_ONLY`, not the suspension; a binding of another owner's at a platform door, the platform's refusal, not "another owner". The erased, discarded and erasing owner still refuse first, at every door.
- **Fixed:** a call with no binding at a file reader's door (`fileRead`, `fileReadChunks`, `fileList`, `fileChanges`) threw a `TypeError` reading `binding.sandbox` before its bind; it is refused `invalid owner principal`, as at every other door (90 lines).

Mutations, each killed: the platform's binding at an owner door no longer refused by its kind (`erasure.test.ts`); a binding not parsed (`grants.test.ts`); an unbound door's step naming its caller allowed (`owner/guards/doors.test.ts`); the sync token presenting the egress kind (`contract/guards/binding.test.ts`); the run door's call untyped (`tsc`, `runs.ts`); a reader door reading an absent binding (`owner.test.ts`); the owner's suspension before the kind (`erasure.test.ts`).
