# T2 as built (2026-11-15)

T2 is lane T's doors half of B23 (d3 §K1): the doors' tests through the owner's front door. It is split at its one hard seam into two PRs. This one (T2a) moves the owner out of the doors' test Worker and every reach into its object with it; `untangle/t2b` builds the doors' real entry and the moves that need product files.

1. **Why the first part is this large.** `runInDurableObject`, `runDurableObjectAlarm`, `evictDurableObject` and a fake `Date` reach only an object of the test's own `main` Worker (`b23_research.md` question 1). So the move of the owner to an auxiliary Worker breaks every reach into its object at once, and no smaller first part is green: the 72 SQL statements, the alarms, the evictions, the owner's clock, `refuseOnce` and the test `OwnerDO` all go in one change.
2. **The owner is an auxiliary Worker, bundled from its real entry** (`doors/vitest.config.ts`), with every binding its config gives it: its `FILES`, `GATEWAY` and `CODEMODE_VM` the real Workers beside it, and `CONTROL` the same namespace the doors' config names. Its `SANDBOX` is the owner suite's fake sandbox Worker, and its `ERASURES` is still T1c-b's `owner-erasures-recorded` queue (item 10).
   - The doors' `OWNER` is the owner gate (`ci/gate.ts` `ownerGate`), in front of the owner Worker's objects. `ownerGate` is the kit's one way to that gate, with every door of the door table; the kit's own config used a second copy of that list, and now uses `ownerGate` too.
   - `test/entry.ts` serves the doors alone: its `OwnerDO` subclass, its six overridden doors, `refuseOnce`'s map and the `OWNER_FILES` cast are deleted.
   - The browser test (`app.e2e.mjs`) runs the owner Worker beside the doors the same way, its `OWNER` the config's own.
   - `test/gates.ts` disarms the owner gate after each test, as the owner suite's does its gates.
3. **The owner gate answers, too** (`ci/gate-worker.ts`): an arming `{ answer }` answers the next matching call in the real one's place, never sending it. It is for state behind an answer that no door can make in a test:
   - a shared app's binding: `inbound.ts` `created` refuses every shared create (`install_proof_required`) until the install flow is built, so no door makes one. `b23_research.md` question 4's "install proof made with the suite's own `ROUTING_MAC_KEY`" names a path the code does not have;
   - a finished restore's step and a wipe's redelivery, which need a whole pinned generation.

   The kit's test holds it: the call is answered as armed and never reaches the real Worker.
4. **Reads are door answers** (question 4): `outside_clients` through `grantList` (`support.ts` `grantsOf`); `owner_admission` through `ownerState` at the platform's door (`admissionOf`); `agent_cards` through `registerProposal` by the card the client's `agent_register` answered; `egress_reservations` through `egressReceipts`; `connections` through the app's `connections/list`; `engine_signins` through `signinList`; the import's state through its own check, run by the consumer (`import.test.ts` `held`).
   - A table door outside `OwnerDoors` is called as the doors call one (`tools.ts` `callDoor`, `support.ts` `ownDoor`).
   - What the doors handed the object is the gate's record (`support.ts` `crossed`): the binding's `authenticated_at` and source, a hint's bytes, a settle's metered spend, a sealed callback that carries no secret, a doorbell, an inbound event's bytes. A test asserts on the record instead of on a row that only shows the record indirectly.
   - `reached(owner)` is what crossed the owner's gate since the last look. `unseen(owner)` forgets a test's own setup's calls (a sign-in's admit) before the call whose reach the test watches.
5. **Writes become refusals, answers or real paths** (question 4):
   - **The owner's clock** (a flow's expiry, a revoke's fence, a lapsed lease, an erased tombstone) becomes the refusal the gate injects. The doors' test holds its status and body, and the owner's suite holds the state behind it: `connections.test.ts`, `grants.test.ts`, `egress.test.ts` and `erasure.test.ts`.
   - **The owner at a ceiling** (1,000 keys, the ledger's hot and unread ceilings) becomes the refusal or answer the gate gives. The owner's suite holds the ceilings (`admit.test.ts`, `ledger-bounds.test.ts`).
   - **The fixtures:** an outside agent is registered by its client's `agent_register` and the owner's `registerAgent` (`support.ts` `registerAgent`), its delivery method its own. `main` is defined through the app's `files/read` and `files/write` in the owner's session, once the seed has landed (`support.ts` `defineMain`), in place of the owner suite's `defineAgent`.
   - **The erasure is split at the door** (question 2): a doors test ends with the outside record and its confirmation. The wipe after the put grace runs on the object's own clock, in the owner's suite (T1c-b's `erasure-support.ts`).
6. **The owner's subjects move to its suite**, beside the code that writes those rows:
   - `runs.test.ts`: the run's row holds its result and no frame, and a session id outside its shape is refused and never kept. A lease keeps its egress token's hash, never the token.
   - `admit.test.ts`: the platform's read of the owner's state answers and changes nothing; a shared app's binding is never made by a door; and an inbound event at the object's door is mailed verbatim, once, its replay deduped and a changed one a conflict, refused `mailbox_full` at the hot ceiling without counting to the breaker, and the breaker trips, tells the owner and re-arms. No owner test reached `inboundAccept` before: the doors' suite was its only test.
   - `restore.test.ts`: a pinned owner's state names its hold.
7. **The doors' tests type `OWNER` as the doors' env does** (C2b's owed note). One tsc program merges every workspace's `Cloudflare.Env`, and the owner suite's declares `OWNER` as its object's class. So the doors' `env.d.ts` declares no `OWNER`, and a doors test reaches it as `doorsEnv.OWNER`, typed `OwnerDoors`.
8. **`secretOf` lives in `ci/workerd.ts`**, the workerd test helpers of more than one workspace. Its one copy is now read by both suites, in place of the doors' import of `owner/test/runs-support.ts`.
9. **Kept as the decision put it:**
   - The import's check test holds the job's diff (`import/check.ts` `differences`) against a bundle changed under the owner's real pages, in place of SQL that changed the owner under the bundle. A lost chunk is that page's answer with `read` null; that the object answers a lost chunk so is `owner/test/import.test.ts`.
   - The run's resumed upload stays one intent in `owner/test/import.test.ts`.
10. **Owed to `untangle/t2b`:**
    - **The doors' real entry** (`main: src/index.ts`, research T2 item 2): the fake upstream and the test OAuth source reach the entry by config, which needs `d2:N49`, `N50` and `N53` in `doors/src` first. With it, T1c-b's `owner-erasures-recorded` queue goes: the owner's `tinyassets-erasure` is consumed by the doors' own `queue` export, and the doors' erasure tests wait for its outside record.
    - **The last seven B23 reaches** (`doors/guards/rules.ts` `OWED`):
      - the owner's constants the doors' tests read: `ERASE_CONFIRM`, `NO_ERASURE`, `OWNER_UNADMITTED`, `PLATFORM_ONLY`, `BILLED_WINDOW_S`, `BODY_BYTES_MAX` and `SESSION_REFUSED`. They move where both sides read them, which is a product move, so no T2a change;
      - the fakes' read-back types (`env.d.ts` → `owner/test/fake-*.ts`).
    - **Answers typed from the contract:** a test types a table door's answer by hand (`ownDoor<T>`), since `OwnerDoors` types those doors `object` or names them not at all.
    - **`d2:N52`, `N84`, `N85` and `N89`**: `pageSize`, the restated strings, and the sleeps. A sleep left in this PR's files is one of N89's, for example `doors-live.test.ts`'s `sent`.
