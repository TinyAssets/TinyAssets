Before, those rules lived in host-side briefs (`brief_common.md`, `brief_lane.md`, `brief_w2.md`, `brief_rvw2.md`), where they drifted from the repo, and nothing in the repo said a process fix comes before product work: d3 §B5 scheduled P6, P7's rest and P9 "after the lanes", and a CI fix was deferred under the cutover rule though every PR runs through CI.

The gates page keeps what list3's P7 gave it: the done-check, the receipt, the queue. The skill names that page once, and says nothing it says. list3's P7 made the gates page the one page, but its pin (1,387 B) cannot hold the skill's rules, and the rulebook's pins only fall.
