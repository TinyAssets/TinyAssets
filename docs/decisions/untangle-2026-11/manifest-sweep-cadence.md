# A sweep and the next nightly generation

R7 reads the retained generations before every listing page. An older export can expire during the listing. Pruning its generation then records another owed sweep, and the next pass has to start listing from the beginning. The nightly generation has a different reason to begin. Its owner has changed and its next spread-out night has arrived. The first generation and an explicit owner export or restore pin also have their own initiation paths.

At main f934871ad and combined 6db9b9e60, Backups.next feeds the owner's one manifest alarm. It returns the earliest of retention expiry, the nightly opening, and any owed sweep when no generation is writing. After the sweep, Backups.run uses the same minimum to decide whether to open a generation. A remaining sweep can therefore open a dirty owner's nightly copy early.

An offline replay used all eighteen migrations, the actual WriteGate, Backups and ChangeLog, two synthetic finished generations, an open export on the older one and an owed sweep. The newest had changed. With the export still open for the second page, both pages kept two generations and the pass opened none. With the export expiring two seconds into the listing, the second page kept only the newest, and the durable sweep count advanced during the listing. The pass then opened a generation. Independent of the retention and sweep work, its scheduled night was 9,602 seconds after the start of the pass. It opened 9,600 seconds early. Both cases had the same outcome on each commit on Node 22 and Node 24. The fixture supplied two synthetic store pages and stopped the unexpected capture at its next namespace request.

The existing R7 workerd test adds a second sweep while the first runs and checks that the second remains owed. Its newest generation is unchanged, so it cannot distinguish a due sweep from a due nightly opening. Verify the dirty-newest case through the owner Worker. Before changing the timing, reconcile the independent first-night candidate, direct pin and export requests, existing writing-generation retry, expired generations, and no-change dormancy. Keep the per-page retained list and the conditional sweep completion. A single alarm can wake for several reasons while each operation consumes only its own due work.

## Local implementation and scope

The schedule still wakes at the earliest of its retention, sweep and capture duties. The runner now uses the nightly date alone to authorize a new automatic capture. It checks the real clock, remaining pass budget, owner halt and restore hold after listing returns. An existing writing generation remains available to the restore that owns it. A restore holding its source without a fresh pointer may release before the next eligible automatic opening.

On main, a two-page sweep crossing UTC midnight stamped the new generation with the previous day's start. A final page taking the remaining pass time could also start the next capture after the budget was exhausted. Local 250ba95fe and combined eff491215 used the actual opening date and deferred exhausted work to the next pass. The two heads passed 40 offline owner/SQLite scenarios across Node 22 and 24. They included the old export expiring between pages, opening at the independent due time, a held restore with and without an explicit writing generation, a released hold, a late erasure mark, midnight and the pass cutoff. These cases checked whether a capture started. They did not depend on a successful remote copy.

The integration uses the first-night design's durable initial date and live-owner admission predicate. Standalone main retains its existing first-night behavior. Sweep traversal across multiple bounded passes and the SDK alarm projection are separate outstanding dependencies, recorded in manifest-sweep-progress.md on its own branch and the physical wake study. The public owner Worker on original f934871ad wrote an extra manifest part during the early-opening case. Exact Worker, integration and recorder evidence is tracked by commit in the cumulative review.

The four regression cases on 2358dba19 passed together through the local owner Worker, as did scoped Node 22 TypeScript. On f934871ad the same conditions each failed at their intended observation. The expired pin caused an early write. A hold acquired during the final page lost its source. A spent pass opened a new generation. A pass spanning UTC midnight with time left used the prior day. Seven ordinary backup Worker controls passed on the same production source at 250ba95fe. Full-project behavior and scheduled offsite return have their own verification.
