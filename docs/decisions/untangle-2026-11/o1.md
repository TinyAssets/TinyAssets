# O1 as built (2026-11-15)

B3's first PR, as `o2.md`'s "B3's shape" (from `o1_research.md`, option (b′)): the runner's types in `owner/src/door.ts`, the body placement in `Bodies`, `Archive` with the stores, the row step `seeded`, the tiers kept at the bind, `call.scope` and `call.universeOf`, and the leaf modules on `<Module>DoorNeeds`. The object's state still satisfies each converted module's `DoorNeeds`, at `HANDLERS` (`DoorsOf<M, OwnerState>`); no second record is made. Owed: `untangle/o1b` (the four proofs, the policy stores, the guard cut), `untangle/o1c` (files, collections, ledger, import) and `untangle/o1d` (runs, launch, signins, restore, `OwnerState` deleted).

1. **`door.ts` holds the runner's types:** `Account`, `Bound`, `Readable`, `Call`, `Door`, `Units`, the handler generics (`DoorName`, `Module`, `CallerOf`, `GateOf`, `HandlerOf`, `DoorsOf`), and the two shapes every `DoorNeeds` picks from: `DoorUnits` (`door`, `quiet`, `synced`, `filing`, `sync`) and `ObjectReads` (`env`, `storage`, `owner`, `control`, `gone`, `discard`, `reset`, `accountAt`, `invoke`). `OwnerState` extends the two. The 27 modules that imported `Call`, `Door` or `Account` from `owner-do.ts` import them from `door.ts`; the unconverted ones still import `OwnerState`, and `grants`, `keys`, `ledger` and `registrations` the request types `GrantRequest` and `RegisterRequest`, which move with their modules in `untangle/o1b`.
2. **`Grant` and `Scope` move to `authority.ts`**, beside `may`, the one check that reads them. `door.ts`'s `Account` names the grant, and only the object imports `grants` (policy-boundary's `MODULES`, a list that only shrinks). `authority.ts`'s private `Triple`, a copy of `Scope`, goes.
3. **A `DoorNeeds` names its module's own store by its class, and each other module's reach as `Pick<Class, "method" | …>`, or, where an import list guards that module (the ledger's importers; policy-boundary's `MODULES`), as the methods' signatures.** So no handler module gains an import a guard refuses, and no signature is copied where tsc can take the class's own. Converted: notices, usage, starter, sandbox-time, relay, erasure, inbound, connections, egress, export and codemode. An `env` reach is the bindings it reads (`Pick<OwnerEnv, …>`). A `DoorNeeds` member the object's state lacks is a tsc error at `HANDLERS` (mutation: a member `missing` in `NoticesDoorNeeds`, refused there).
4. **The body placement is `Bodies`' (`placing`, `reading`, `rereading`, `rereadingPage`)**, over the files and the gate's `write` it already holds; `#placing`'s `chunk_deleting` retry moves with it, with its reason. Their `synced` option goes: the five doors that passed it (`collectionWrite`, `collectionDelete`, `roomPost`, `importRows`'s rows, `inboundAccept`) end with `try { … } finally { await o.sync() }`. The sync now runs after a page's fill and on a failure too, so no ring is left unarmed. `roomPost`'s `busyRings` (a sync on `storage_busy`) is that `finally`, and goes. `placing`'s options object, left with `shown` alone, becomes that parameter.
5. **`PAGE_BYTES` and `READ_TRIES` move to `bodies.ts`**, beside `READ_PAGE_BYTES` and `WINDOWS_BYTES`: the placement reads both, and `pages.ts` already imports `bodies.ts`, so a value import back would make the runtime cycle `windowEnd`'s note warns of. Their importers are repointed.
6. **`Archive` is built by `ownerStores`.** Its parts are the stores and the gate's `write`; its page read is `Bodies.rereadingPage`, and its unused `storage` part goes. `archive-read.test.ts` reaches it as `storesOn(state).archive`, not a cast stand-in.
7. **The row step `seeded`** (`contract/doors.json`, until `untangle/o2b` splits the table): on its eight rows (`fileList`, `fileText`, `fileSearch`, `fileFetch`, `fileAppend`, `fileWrite`, `fileEdit`, `outsideAgents`) the runner checks the caller's authority in a unit, then awaits the starter seed, then calls the handler. `#seededFor` and `OwnerState.seededFor` go. `doorProblems` refuses `seeded` on a row whose gate is not `units`.
8. **The tiers are kept at the bind alone.** `#bind` keeps the numbers a binding hands, with the first numbers' seed sync, after its every refusal. The account step's keep, the import bind's, `roomPost`'s (`poster`, which an account's bind had already kept) and `inboundAccept`'s go, and so does `OwnerState.keepTiers`. An inbound event's numbers are now kept at its first unit, a deduped or no-op event's too; an imported owner's first numbers make its seed due at the import, as an account's do. A binding that hands none (a sandbox's, a run's, the platform's) keeps what is kept, as before.
9. **`call.scope(capability, center?, agent?)` and `call.universeOf()`** are the runner's `#scope` and `#universe` on the call's account; `OwnerState.scope` and `.universe` go, and the ledger's `mailAuthority` and `mailsAgent` take the call. **`held` stays**, against the research's "takes in today's `held`": `wakeEvents` and `wakeSubscribe` (`scope: "handler"`) hold their row's capability where the handler names, and through `scope` their handlers would spell that capability again, a copy of the row's value. Left for the coordinator; `untangle/o1c` (the ledger) takes it if the decision stands.
10. **`signinOwned(o, id)`**, a helper taking the whole state for one store's read, is `Signins.owned(id)`.
11. **Surplus removed on the way:** `OwnerDO`'s unread `#log`; `inboundEvent`'s and `actorOf`'s unused state parameter; `ledger.ts`'s unused `Binding` import; `contract/src/egress-scope.ts`'s unused `count` import (outside lane O's files: one line).
12. **Guards:** `write-gate` reads the placement from `bodies.ts` and the seed step from `#call`; a handler's first statement may be a `try` whose first begins in the gate; `o.bodies.<placement>` are entries and their units' arguments. Its probe of a handler awaiting `o.seededFor` goes with the member, replaced by one whose `try` returns a placement.
