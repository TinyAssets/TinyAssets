# shard-2-time as built (2026-11-16)

`untangle/required-checks` found `required-tests shard 2/6` near its 30-minute watchdog and the `required-tests` aggregate near the queue's 35-minute check timeout. Research first (Fact 14), read-only, from the Actions API.

1. **Measured.** 24 merge-group runs of `tests.yml`, 43705715555 to 43947500213 (43711240447's job list returned 502). Four ran the whole surface (43940676068, 43939779247, 43769559562, 43705715555): shard 2 took 24.4, 25.8, 26.9 and 24.9 minutes of job, the other five 4.4 to 9.8, and `required-tests` reported 26.2, 27.7, 28.6 and 26.7 minutes after the run was created. The 20 selective runs' shards all took 3.3 to 6.9. From those four runs' `junit-required-tests`: shard 2 held 1,196 to 1,306 s of tests, of 1,967 to 2,099 s in all. `tests/test_owner_volumes_proof.py` took 483 to 534 s (one case, `test_owner_volumes_real_mounts`, 477 s in 43940676068's log), `tests/test_deploy_bundle_transaction.py` 453 to 504 s across its cases and `tests/test_owner_router.py` 156 to 182 s; all three are root-venue proofs, and shard 2's root venue alone ran 1,169 s in 43940676068. A shard's job is its tests plus 4.5 to 5.1 minutes (the oracle image build, the checkout, the plan and the upload).
2. **The cause: a stale table, not the packing rule.** The shards are already packed by measured file time, longest first (`ci_required_tests.pack`). `.github/test-durations.json` was last written on 2026-10-21, with 134 files: the volume proof was not in it (it counted as the median, under a second) and the deploy-bundle proof read 36.7 s. Both landed on shard 2. The refresher read the last five successful merge groups, which are now mostly selective; a selective run's junit holds only its selection, so a refresh from them drops every file they left out.
3. **#5646's runner-speed calibration does not reach the Python legs.** It calibrates each Vitest process (`platform/ci/classes.ts`) and re-derived the platform watchdogs at about 6 times their slowest leg. The Python shards have no per-test budget to scale and their 30-minute watchdog was not re-derived; nothing in them measures the runner.
4. **Weighed.**
   - *Longest-first over the whole surface with a fresh table.* The slowest shard would be the volume proof alone, 483 to 534 s. But that shard's user venue then owns nothing: on the whole surface its pytest collects no case and exits 5, and the shard runs 2 cases against its floor of 260 (`MIN_RAN_FLOORS["shard"]`). A per-shard floor in cases and a pack in seconds disagree whenever one file is a quarter of the suite.
   - *Add shards.* The floor is one 477 s case; a seventh shard moves the slowest from 621 s to 610, at the cost of a seventh image build per merge group.
   - *A leg of its own for the heavy proof.* The same floor, plus a job, an artifact and an aggregate branch of its own: a special case.
   - *Raise a watchdog or the ruleset's timeout.* Not a fix; the ruleset is the founder's (`docs/ops/merge-queue-ruleset.json`) and is unchanged.
5. **Built: each venue packed on its own, from whole-surface runs only.**
   - `_packed` packs the user-venue files and the root-venue files (`ROOT_TEST_FILES`) each longest-first across all N shards. A shard runs the two venues as two pytest processes one after the other, so each venue's time spreads evenly and the root proofs' minutes cannot take a shard's share of the user venue. Every shard keeps 402 to 985 cases (floor 260) and the slowest shard's tests take 569 to 621 s in the four runs' junits, against 1,196 to 1,306 before. A file the table does not know counts as its venue's median.
   - `refresh_test_durations.py` reads the last five successful merge groups whose `affected-selection` is `ALL` (`selection_entries`, the one reader), searching back 200 runs.
   - The table is rewritten by that script from the four runs above (`--junit`): 234 files, 2,007 s.
6. **Projected.** The slowest shard: 569 to 621 s of tests plus 4.5 to 5.1 minutes, so 14 to 15.5 minutes of its 30 (at most 52%); `required-tests` reports about 1.8 minutes after its last shard, so 16 to 17.5 of 35 (at most 50%). Both stay within the 20% margin on a runner up to about 1.5 times slower than these, and inside their limits up to about 1.9 times.
7. **Held.** `test_every_shard_runs_its_user_venue_and_plans_within_half_its_watchdog` (`tests/test_tests_workflow.py`): the table's packing gives every shard a measured user-venue file, and no shard plans more than half its watchdog, which leaves the image build and a fifth; a refresh that shows a shard near its watchdog fails it. Mutations: one pack over both venues fails the first assertion; the root proofs packed onto one shard (1,574 s) fail the second. `test_the_durations_refresh_reads_only_whole_surface_runs`: of three `ALL` runs and one selective one, the first two `ALL` runs' junits come back. Mutation: the selection test dropped returns the selective run.
8. **Found and left.**
   - *A runner five times slower* (`budgets-variance.md`) fits no packing: the one 477 s case plus the image build is about 12.5 minutes, 62 at five times, and a selective shard's 6.9 minutes would be 35. None appeared in the 144 shard jobs read here. Whether the Python legs need slow-runner coverage before the cutover deletes them is research of its own: `untangle/python-slow-runner`.
   - *The table goes stale again* when a root proof grows by minutes. The new test fails on the refresh that shows it; between refreshes the cost is balance, never coverage, and a shard at its watchdog turns main red loudly.
