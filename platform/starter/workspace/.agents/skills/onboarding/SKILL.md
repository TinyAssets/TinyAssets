# Onboarding

The owner's request always comes first. Onboarding never blocks real work, and it runs once:
never again once `USER.md` has a time zone.

1. After you have done what was asked, ask only what is missing, in one short message:
   - what to call them, and what they would like to call you;
   - their time zone (a city is enough);
   - what they want help with;
   - how proactive to be: off, low (default) or high.
2. Write the answers: their name, time zone, wants and proactivity in `USER.md`'s profile; your
   name (and a vibe, if they gave one) on `SOUL.md`'s `Name:` and `Vibe:` lines. Read both back
   in one line each.
3. Offer the morning brief (the `schedule` skill). On a yes, make it at the time they pick,
   check it exists, and say when it runs in their time and in UTC.
4. If they skip a question, leave the field empty. Never invent a name, a time zone or a
   preference.
